Common questions

How do I add or remove currencies?

Tap the search button on the home screen to choose currencies. To remove one, swipe left on it in the list.

How often are rates updated?

Rates are refreshed several times a day. The home screen shows when they were last updated.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The most recently downloaded rates are used until you're back online.

常見問題

怎麼新增或移除幣別？

在首頁點搜尋按鈕即可選擇幣別。要移除時，在列表中把該幣別往左滑。

匯率多久更新一次？

匯率每天會更新數次，首頁會顯示最後更新時間。

離線時能用嗎？

可以。在恢復連線前，會使用最近一次下載的匯率。
