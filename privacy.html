Exchanger Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026 · 繁體中文

Exchanger is a currency converter developed by CHIH YEN CHEN. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data we don't collect

Exchanger has no user accounts. It does not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, or any other personal information. It does not show ads and does not track you across apps or websites.

Data stored on your device

Your selected currencies and the most recently downloaded exchange rates are stored only on your device so the app works offline. This data is never sent to us. Deleting the app removes it.

Exchange rates

To download exchange rates, the app connects to Google Firebase using an anonymous identifier that is not linked to you. Rates are sourced from ExchangeRate-API; the app does not send any personal data to it.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics collects crash and diagnostic information (such as the device model, iOS version, and the state of the app at the time of the crash) so we can fix problems. This information is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking or advertising. See Firebase privacy information.

Children

Exchanger does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Exchanger 隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 11 月 5 日

Exchanger 是由 CHIH YEN CHEN 開發的匯率換算 app。本政策說明 app 會處理哪些資料。

我們不收集的資料

Exchanger 沒有帳號系統，不收集你的姓名、電子郵件、聯絡人、位置或任何其他個人資料。App 沒有廣告，也不會跨 app 或網站追蹤你。

儲存在你裝置上的資料

你選擇的幣別和最近下載的匯率只會存在你的裝置上，讓 app 在離線時也能使用。這些資料不會傳送給我們，刪除 app 時會一併移除。

匯率資料

為了下載匯率，app 會以一個不與你個人連結的匿名識別碼連線到 Google Firebase。匯率來源是 ExchangeRate-API，app 不會傳送任何個人資料給它。

當機報告

App 當機時，Firebase Crashlytics 會收集當機與診斷資訊（例如裝置型號、iOS 版本，以及當機時 app 的狀態），讓我們能修正問題。這些資訊不會與你的身分連結，也不會用於追蹤或廣告。詳見 Firebase 隱私權資訊。

兒童

Exchanger 不會刻意收集任何人的個人資料，包括兒童。

政策變更

本政策如有變更，會在此頁面公布更新後的版本與日期。
